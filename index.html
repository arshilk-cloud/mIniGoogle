Why PageRank Changed Everything

In the early web, pizza-pizza-pizza.com gamed traditional search engines simply by repeating the word 10 times. Google's PageRank solved this by treating hyperlinks as votes of confidence. Because 4 different food and recipe websites linked directly to best-pizza-pune.com, it rightfully earned the #1 spot, while unreferenced spam sank to the bottom.
